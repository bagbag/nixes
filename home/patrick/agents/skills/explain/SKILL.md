# Explain

Explain the argument's subject, otherwise your last message: what it is,
where it lives or runs, why it matters to the current discussion and what it
changes for the decision at hand. Verify it in its source first (board item,
file, doc); say so when that changes your earlier content or
recommendations. Use plain words and short sentences; define each term, id
and earlier artifact at first use; name items in plain words with ids in
brackets. End with an offer to ask.
