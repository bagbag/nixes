## Decision discipline

Use this discipline for consequential choices between materially different
options.

### Ground the comparison

- Freeze the alternatives, decision criteria, verified premises, unresolved
  facts, and conditions that would change each recommendation.
- Resolve decision-changing facts from authoritative project and dependency
  sources.
- Separate required properties from proposed mechanisms. Compare mechanisms by
  how well they satisfy those properties and the user's quality criterion.
- For coordination and orchestration choices, distinguish authoritative domain
  state from operational execution state. Inspect the native platform's actual
  ownership, identity, lifecycle, integrity, isolation, failure, retry,
  timeout, routing, transaction, recovery, observability, operability, and
  extension semantics before adding parallel state or coordination.
- Identify the consequential architectural property before descending into an
  implementation preference.
- State the ranking, decisive rationale, strongest downside, and flip condition.

### Reconcile disagreement

- Apply reconciliation only when the disagreement could materially change the
  decision or downstream work.
- Give each reviewer the other's strongest argument in a neutral frame.
- A changed recommendation must identify new evidence, a corrected premise, or
  an explicit reweighting of trade-offs.
- Treat position-swapping without resolved premises as continued disagreement.
  Resolve verifiable uncertainty first; use fresh neutral adjudication when
  consequential judgment remains.
- A compromise, hybrid, or other synthesized alternative is a new option. Test
  it neutrally against the original criteria and determine whether it preserves
  the parents' benefits or merely combines their costs.

### Synthesize

- Do not force consensus or decide by majority. Synthesize from verified facts,
  explicit trade-offs, and independent judgment. Treat agreement after shared
  framing or a lead-authored amendment as correlated evidence.
- Preserve material disagreement, distinguish facts from preferences, and
  return genuine trade-offs to the decision owner with a recommendation.
